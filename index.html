Our goal


With our Cansat project, we would like to measure the gust intensity and turbulence on certain altitudes. Our mission consists of 2 parts: the primary mission, which measures air temperature and air pressure. All the teams that participate in this project have the same primary mission. The secondary mission is different for every team. Team Voytech will measure linear acceleration and the horizontal displacement. Altogether, all the movements our Cansat makes are captured by our sensors. These movements are caused by its surroundings, so by that we can capture gust intensity and turbulence. On top of this, we will also use a spinning disk inside of the CanSat which will stop the spin the CanSat gets from when it is let out of the rocket.
